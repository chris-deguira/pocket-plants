Small plants, simple care

Pocket Plants is a tiny guide to six easy houseplants, with light, watering, and pet-safety notes in one place.

Featured plants

Pothos

Epipremnum aureum

Light
Low to bright indirect
Water
Every 1 to 2 weeks, when the top inch of soil is dry
Difficulty
Easy

A trailing vine that forgives missed waterings and grows in almost any room.

How it works

  1. Pick a plant that fits the light available in the room.
  2. Check the watering rhythm and the pet-safety note.
  3. Use the planner on the care page to see when water is due next.
