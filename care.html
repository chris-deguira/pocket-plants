Common questions

How can overwatering be spotted?

Yellowing lower leaves, soft stems, and soil that stays wet for days are the usual signs. Let the soil dry further before the next watering.

Do all plants need a pot with drainage holes?

Drainage holes let extra water escape, which protects the roots. A decorative pot without holes works best as an outer cover for a plain pot that has them.

Should leaves be misted?

Misting raises humidity only briefly. Grouping plants together or using a tray of pebbles and water helps ferns more than misting does.
